Product overview

One platform for the work between selling, buying and stock.

Most businesses already keep records of sales, purchases, stock and payments. The difficulty is that these records live in different places: a register, a spreadsheet, a billing tool, a message thread. OneSync ERP brings them onto one platform, so that what is recorded once can be used everywhere it matters.

How we build

Eight principles behind the product

{{icon:bolt}}

Simple by default

A small business should not have to learn ERP concepts or see features it does not need. Guided setup and clear terms come first.

{{icon:layers}}

Depth when needed

Advanced functions are available through levels and optional modules, and they do not make everyday work harder.

{{icon:link}}

One source of truth

A customer, product or payment is recorded once. Every part of the product uses the same record.

{{icon:route}}

Real workflows

A specialist process is built as a proper workflow with its own steps and records, not imitated with extra fields.

Settings before custom code

Common needs are handled through supported settings. OneSync does not maintain a separate version of the software for each customer.

Editions and growth

Not every business needs the same level of control.

OneSync is one product with different levels of depth. As your team, locations and controls grow, you add capability without moving to a different system or losing history. The level you need depends on how your business operates, not on turnover alone.

Essential

Run the daily business

Best for: owner-led or small-team businesses with straightforward sales, purchase, stock and payment work.

What it adds: guided setup, a focused menu, standard reports, basic roles, simple stock and payments.

Growth

Coordinate the team

Best for: businesses adding staff, branches, warehouses and approvals.

What it adds: more detailed permissions, approval steps, multiple locations, more pricing and credit controls, recurring transactions, and more import, export and integration options.

Advanced

Control complex operations

Best for: businesses with specialist processes or tighter governance.

What it adds: deeper controls and traceability, specialist modules, and more advanced reporting and integration options.

What stays the same at every level. A transaction is never silently miscalculated, and stock is never double-counted, because of the level you are on. Higher levels add planning, control and analysis. They do not add accuracy, because accuracy is built into every level.

Growing should not mean starting again

{{icon:trend}}

Moving up

Moving to a higher level keeps your company records, customer and product records, past transactions, attachments, document numbers and history. The new capabilities switch on. Where a new feature needs setup, such as adding a warehouse, we guide you through it with a checklist. Old transactions are never silently reinterpreted.

{{icon:undo}}

Moving down

Moving to a lower level never deletes your history or rewrites past transactions. Before the change, you are told which workflows will be affected. You keep the ability to view your records and export them.

What you can adjust yourself

Flexible within safe limits

OneSync lets you adapt the product to your business without breaking the records that keep your stock and accounts correct.

You can adjust

  • Company, branch and warehouse settings
  • Numbering formats and document branding within the available templates
  • Default units, warehouses, price lists and payment terms
  • Approval limits and supported approval chains
  • Optional steps in a workflow, where the step does not change what the transaction means
  • Report filters, saved views and dashboard preferences
  • Custom fields with defined types, checks and access rules
  • Notification preferences

Changed with extra care

Tax treatment, accounting settings, stock valuation, permissions to post or reverse, and rules that change how orders, payments or document lifecycles work are changed with the right permissions, checks and a history of the change.

Not allowed

Running custom code in settings, silently changing posted transactions, unlogged changes to financial or stock rules, skipping required checks, or using unrelated fields to imitate a different industry process.

Settings are flexible within safe limits, so you can adapt OneSync without breaking the records your stock and accounts depend on.

Control and security

Control that grows with your team

Your records are your business. OneSync gives each person access to what their role needs, separates one company’s data from another’s, and keeps a history of important actions.

{{icon:users}}

Roles and permissions

Give each person access that matches their role, instead of sharing one login. Restricted actions are blocked in the application itself, not just hidden on screen.

{{icon:building}}

Company separation

Records stay within the right company. A user working in one company cannot accidentally create or view records in another.

{{icon:history}}

History of actions

Important actions and changes keep who did them and when, so you can investigate when something looks wrong.

{{icon:undo}}

Corrections, not erasure

Posted records are corrected through returns, reversals or adjustments. Nothing is silently edited or deleted.

{{icon:check}}

Approvals

Where a business requires it, a transaction can wait for an approval before it moves on.

Your data

Your data stays yours

You can bring your existing records in when you start, and take your core business records out if you ever leave. We would rather you stay because the software works for you.

  • OneSync is designed to import customers, suppliers, products and opening balances when you start.
  • A documented way to export your core business data is part of every level of the product.
  • Moving to a lower level or cancelling does not erase your historical records.
