What is OneSync ERP?

OneSync ERP is a modular business management platform that connects day-to-day workflows for Indian businesses: sales, purchasing, inventory and payment follow-up, on shared records.

Who is it for?

Small and growing businesses with connected sales, purchasing, inventory or operational management needs. Trading and distribution is the core focus. See the Solutions page for where it fits and where it does not.

Does it include accounting?

Accounting needs vary. Some businesses want financial records close to their operations, and others keep their existing accounting software. Tell us how you work and we will be clear about what fits before you rely on OneSync for accounting or statutory reporting.

Does it support GST?

GST, e-invoicing and e-way bill needs differ from business to business, and general invoicing does not prove every statutory workflow is covered. Tell us what you need and we will be straightforward about what OneSync covers. Please confirm tax requirements with your accountant.

Can I see the product before choosing it?

Yes. Request a demo and describe the workflow you care about. We will walk through the relevant parts and say plainly what sits outside the product.
