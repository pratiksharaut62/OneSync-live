Solutions

Fit depends on how your business works.

Business type and business size are two different questions. OneSync answers the first by adding specialist workflows only when a business genuinely needs them, and the second by offering more or less depth of control. A small manufacturer may need only simple purchasing and stock. A larger trader may need more users and locations but straightforward workflows. So we look at your real operation, not just your turnover.

Trading and distribution

Core focus

This is where OneSync is strongest. A trading or distribution business buys stock from suppliers, holds it, and sells it to customers on agreed terms. That makes sales, purchasing, stock and dues tightly connected, and that connection is what OneSync is built around.

{{icon:target}}

What this business usually needs

  • Customers and suppliers with their pricing and terms
  • Orders that check stock and move through dispatch to invoice
  • Receiving that records what actually arrived
  • Stock you can trace, with reorder thresholds
  • Receipts and payments linked to the right invoices and bills
  • Pending orders, stock and dues in a few clear reports

If you work with price tiers, credit limits, multi-location dispatch or replenishment planning, tell us. We will explain what is covered today.

Manufacturing

Partial fit

A manufacturer buys materials, holds stock and sells finished goods. OneSync can support the purchasing, stock and sales side of that work. Production planning, bills of materials, work orders and quality control are outside the core today.

What fits today: materials purchasing, stock of materials and finished goods, and sales of finished goods.

What sits outside the core: bills of materials, routings, production orders, material consumption, work in progress, quality checks, scrap and rework, and production costing. These are specialist workflows with their own records, and they need to be built properly rather than imitated with extra fields.

If production planning is central to how you work, tell us what you need. We will say plainly whether OneSync fits today.

Retail and FMCG

Partial fit

Retail and fast-moving consumer goods businesses need sound back-office control of stock, purchasing and supplier dues. OneSync can support that. The counter side is outside the core: billing at a till with barcode scanning, cash drawers, shifts, promotions and fast checkout.

What fits today: back-office stock and movements, purchasing and receiving, supplier bills and dues.

If you need a billing counter, tell us how your stores work and we will be clear about what is and is not covered.

Services, projects and other businesses

Service and project businesses, and other specialist industries, can talk to us about fit. Services work such as projects, work logs, timesheets and service billing is a different shape of business from stock-based trading, and we would rather say so than overpromise.

If your business works differently from the examples above, describe how, and we will tell you plainly whether it fits and what we would need to learn.
