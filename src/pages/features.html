Capabilities

What OneSync covers

OneSync is organised around the work a trading or distribution business does every day. Each area below uses the same customer, supplier and product records, so a change made once is visible everywhere it matters.

  • Customers and suppliers
  • Products and units
  • Sales and orders
  • Purchasing and receiving
  • Inventory
  • Payments and dues
  • Reports
  • Control and platform
{{icon:users}}

One record for every customer and supplier

Keep each customer and supplier in one place, with contacts, addresses, pricing and transaction history. When you create an order or a purchase, pick the record instead of typing the details again. Changes to a record follow clear rules and never silently rewrite transactions that were already posted.

{{icon:check}}

What you can do

  • Keep customer and supplier records with contacts, addresses and terms
  • Reuse those records in every order, purchase, invoice and payment
  • Be warned before a consequential transaction is completed with required details missing
  • Be warned about likely duplicate records
  • Apply customer-specific price lists and credit limits where your business uses them
{{icon:box}}

Products with a stable identity

Each product has one identity and a defined unit of measure, so a quantity means the same thing in purchasing, stock and sales. Where a product is bought in one unit and sold in another, the conversion is explicit.

{{icon:check}}

What you can do

  • Keep a catalogue of products and services with clear item types
  • Define units of measure and explicit conversions between them
  • Maintain price lists with effective dates
  • Avoid the same item appearing twice under slightly different names
{{icon:cart}}

From quotation to invoice, on one thread

Create a quotation or order, confirm it, check stock, record delivery and raise the invoice. Each document links to the one before it, so you can trace a sale from its first record to its payment. Fulfilment, invoicing and payment are tracked separately, because they do not always move together.

{{icon:check}}

What you can do

  • Create quotations, sales orders and invoices that stay linked to each other
  • Apply pricing and discount rules consistently, with restrictions that unauthorised users cannot bypass
  • Record partial deliveries and partial invoices against the same order
  • Hold or approve an order when a credit limit or discount limit is exceeded
  • Record returns and cancellations with a link to the original sale
{{icon:clipboard}}

What you ordered, what arrived, what you owe

Raise a purchase order, receive goods against it and record the supplier’s bill. OneSync keeps the quantity you ordered separate from the quantity that actually arrived, and treats a short, excess or damaged delivery as an exception to deal with, not a figure to overwrite.

{{icon:check}}

What you can do

  • Create purchase orders, goods receipts and supplier bills that stay linked
  • Record received quantities separately from ordered quantities
  • Handle short, excess, damaged or rejected deliveries as visible exceptions
  • View supplier price and purchase history
  • Require approval for purchases where your business needs it
  • Record returns to suppliers with a link to the original receipt
{{icon:warehouse}}

Stock you can explain

See what you hold, where you hold it, and the movements that explain every change. Each balance traces back to a receipt, a dispatch, a transfer, a count or a return. Low-stock alerts help you reorder before you run out.

What you can do

  • See stock on hand by item and location
  • Trace any balance to the movements and documents behind it
  • Record adjustments with a reason and a responsible user
  • Transfer stock between locations, where you use more than one
  • Compare a physical count with recorded stock and review the differences
  • Set reorder thresholds and receive low-stock alerts
{{icon:rupee}}

Know who owes you and whom you owe

Record receipts and payments and link them to the invoices and bills they settle. See which customers are overdue and which supplier bills are due. Partial payments, overpayments and unallocated receipts are visible, so nothing sits unexplained.

What you can do

  • Record receipts and payments against documents
  • See each document as unpaid, partly paid or paid
  • View customer and supplier outstanding amounts with due dates and source documents
  • Allocate a payment across several documents, and see unallocated amounts
  • Record business expenses

Reports are built from the same records people work with every day. Summary figures link to the documents behind them, so you can find out why a number is what it is. Each person sees an overview of the work and figures relevant to their role.

About OneSync

What is OneSync ERP?

OneSync ERP is a modular business management platform that connects day-to-day workflows for Indian businesses: sales, purchasing, inventory and payment follow-up, on shared records.

Is OneSync one product or several?

One platform. Different levels of depth and specialist workflows are added to the same product, so there is no separate software to switch to as you grow.

How is OneSync different from billing software?

A billing tool records invoices. OneSync also connects the surrounding work: orders, receiving, stock movements, payments and what is still pending.

Can I change how it works for my business?

You can adjust supported settings such as numbering, document templates, price lists, approval limits, custom fields and notification preferences. We do not build a separate version of the software for each customer.

Fit

Which businesses is OneSync ERP for?

Small and growing businesses with connected sales, purchasing, inventory or operational management needs. Trading and distribution is the core focus. See the Solutions page for where it fits and where it does not.

Does my turnover decide whether OneSync fits?

No. Fit depends on your workflow, your current systems, your exceptions and your reporting needs.

We are a manufacturer. Can we use OneSync?

For purchasing, materials, stock and sales, possibly yes. Production planning, bills of materials and quality control are outside the core. Tell us what you need.

We run retail shops. Can we use OneSync?

For back-office stock, purchasing and supplier dues, possibly yes. Billing counters are outside the core.

Can I use it with more than one location?

Tell us how many locations you run and how stock moves between them, and we will explain what is supported today.

Finance and compliance

Does OneSync include accounting?

Accounting needs vary. Some businesses want financial records close to their operations, and others keep their existing accounting software. Tell us how you work and we will be clear about what fits before you rely on OneSync for accounting or statutory reporting.

Does OneSync support GST, e-invoicing or e-way bills?

GST and e-invoicing needs differ from business to business, and general invoicing does not prove every statutory workflow is covered. Tell us what you need and we will be straightforward about what OneSync covers. Please confirm tax requirements with your accountant.

Can I connect OneSync to my accounting software?

If you use accounting software today, tell us which one. We will explain how that fits.

Can I see my stock value in rupees?

OneSync shows stock quantities and movements. Tell us what stock reporting you need and we will explain what is available.
