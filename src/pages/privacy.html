Privacy

Last updated: {{site.lastReviewed}}

This page explains how OneSync ERP handles the details you give us when you visit this website, send a message or book a demo.

Important: please do not put customer data, financial records or other sensitive business information in either form. We only need the details listed below to reply to you.

1. What we collect

When you send a message through the contact form, we collect:

  • Required: your name, work email and your question.
  • Optional: your phone number, business name and what your business does.

When you book a demo, we collect:

  • Required: your name, work email and phone number.
  • Optional: your business name, what your business does, the number of people who would use the system, the number of locations, what you use today, which workflow matters most, and anything else you tell us.

2. Why we collect it

  • To reply to your question or arrange your demo.
  • To prepare a demo around the workflow you describe.
  • To understand what kinds of businesses and questions we hear about.

We use your details only to respond to your request. We do not sell them.

3. Who can see it

Access is limited to the people at OneSync who respond to messages and demo requests, and the service that delivers form submissions to us.

4. This website

This site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page loads. We do not currently set cookies or run analytics on this site. If that changes, we will update this page first.

5. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you have sent us.

6. Contact us

Questions about how we handle your details? Send us a message and we will reply.
