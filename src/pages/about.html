About

Practical business software for Indian businesses.

We believe business software should fit the way a company actually works, whether it is a small owner-run business or a growing team with several locations.

Our purpose

Software should fit the way you work, and no more complex than that.

We want each business to have the workflows, controls and flexibility its operations call for, and no more complexity than that.

What we are building. One platform that connects the work of selling, buying, holding stock and getting paid, so that the records are right and the picture is clear.

How we work

Four ways we work with businesses

  1. Start with your workflow

    We look at how you actually work before suggesting how the software should.

  2. Be plain about limits

    We tell you what sits outside the product.

  3. Keep it simple

    A small business should not need to learn ERP.

  4. Grow with you

    Adding depth should not mean starting again.
